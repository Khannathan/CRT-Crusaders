# CRT Crusaders: Order Theme Bible

> **Owned by ChatGPT, the art lead (since 2026-11-25).** Rewrite and reimagine freely. The locked specs in `specs/` win over anything here; see `specs/LOCKS.md`.

Use this file with the roster Sister cards, class-bible.md and ASSETS_GUIDE.md. It tells you what each of the ten Orders looks, feels and sounds like, so every Sister reads as her Order at a glance.

## How to use this with ChatGPT

1. No Sister is locked: all existing art is being replaced.
2. Before drawing a Sister, read her Order's section here. Her roster card wins on anything personal (face, hair, marks, weapon). This file wins on anything the Order owns (cloth colours, metal, head motif, motif shape, hem, splash place).
3. Who decides what: **Order** = cloth colours, metal finish, head motif, motif shape, hem, splash setting. **Class** = silhouette and weapon. **Sister** = face, hair, skin, marks, weapon. **Rank** = mortal or divine presence, silhouette and the scale of her miracle.
4. **Colour rule:** the Order's lead colour covers about 60% of her cloth, the shared neutrals (steel, bronze, parchment, near-black shadow) about 30%, the trim colour about 10%. On cloth the lead colour is the lit value of a 3-step ramp whose darker steps sink to the shared shadow #211b26, so colours read grimy, never pastel or neon.
5. **The 64 px test:** shrink the sprite to 64 px tall. Someone who knows the ten Orders must name hers from colour and head shape alone.

## Standing art notes (Aaron, 2026-11-23)

These apply to every Sister and win over anything softer elsewhere in this file.

1. **URs are gods of their Order.** The Limited UR is her Order's Patron Deity, its highest being; the two URs are Deities. SSRs are Demigoddesses. Both must read as clearly divine and far beyond any R or SR (see the rank ladder below).
2. **Full hair, always (Aaron, 2026-11-24).** No Sister has a shaved, undercut, buzzed or tonsured hairstyle: use braids, long or tied-back hair, short crops or hair under veils and helms instead.
2b. **Fanservice, always.** Every Sister is video-game hot and shows cleavage or underboob, built into her Order's habit and her class armour (a plunging sternum cut on a breastplate, a short plate that stops above the ribs, a laced V neckline, a keyhole or underboob cut-out, a corset). Her card names which one. Keep it non-explicit gacha fanservice: no nudity, and every Sister stays a young adult of 21 to 29.
3. **Dark, gritty, occult zeal.** Lean hard into inquisition, crusade, heresy and pyres: scripture tattoos, wax purity seals, heretic chains, brands, blood-scrawled verses, soot and smoke. Every card names one zeal mark; every splash has candle or fire light against black, with blood, wax, chains or smoke in the scene.

4. **Rank ladder: Commander (R) to Legend (SR) to Demigoddess (SSR) to Deity (UR).** Each step is a big visual leap. R and SR are mortal heroes, already formidable; SSR and UR are clearly divine. Her class still sets her armour weight; her rank sets its grandeur. Fanservice and zeal marks scale with rank.

7. **Grimdark zealot vibe (Aaron, 2026-11-24).** Every Order is a fanatical zealot army in a war without end, with no modern or future technology. Medieval steel, fire, wax and faith only (crossbows, siege engines and bells are fine): heavy gothic plate etched with prayers, wax purity seals with ribbon scrolls on every armour plate, scripture branded on skin and banners, consecrated fire poured from censers and brands instead of any gun or engine, cathedral-sized relics carried into battle, skull and halo motifs, battle hymns screamed not sung. The mood is grand, gothic and doomed: they will die for the faith and smile doing it. Borrow the feeling only; never copy another setting's names, symbols or designs.

5. **Names grow with rank.** Every Sister is her Roman name plus a rank epithet from her card: a war title for a Commander ("Captain of the Lantern Wall"), a famous deed for a Legend ("Who Held the Black Gate"), divine parentage for a Demigoddess ("Daughter of the Unsleeping Flame"), a god-name and domain for a Deity ("the Amber Watcher, Goddess of Walls"). Let the epithet steer the drawing: a Captain of the Lantern Wall carries the wall's lantern; a Daughter of the Unsleeping Flame burns.

7. **The Wrathful Host (Aaron, 2026-11-24).** The Sisters are the Church's living weapons, and the closer to God, the more they look like biblical angels made dark: wheels of eyes, many wings, burning crowns, scripture fire. It grows with rank. **R:** angel iconography only (wing-shaped pauldrons, an eye-in-a-wheel painted on her shield, seraph heraldry). **SR:** first touches (iron-feather pauldrons, a glowing ring-of-eyes sigil, a feather-edged cape, a faint aura). **SSR:** half-transfigured: two wings of her Order's feathers with eyes opening along them, a partial wheel-halo of eyes, scripture fire on her skin. **UR:** seraph-tier: six wings (two framing her head, two spread, two folded low), burning wheels of eyes turning behind her, a crown of flame, tears of gold wax. Her face always stays human and beautiful (Confessors of Glass stays faceless): extra eyes go on wings, halos and armour, never on her face. On the sprite, wings stay folded tight and wheels small behind her head; the splash shows them full.

## Iron Vigil

### Gatewardens

Aaron chose the gatehouse as the Order’s visual identity. A Gatewarden is recognizable by a squared outer edge, black iron against bone-white cloth, and an ember glimpsed beyond the wall. Pick one piece of gate hardware and give it a real job on the costume. A hinge fastens a mantle. A square iron plate protects the arm holding a shield. Keep heavy bars, gate teeth and locking mechanisms at architectural scale in the scene.

Fit the silhouette to the Sister’s duty. A Sentinel can bear the width of a parapet across a shield and leading shoulder. A Hospitaller needs room to bend and lift. Give her squared folds of split wool and a hinged clasp on a roll of dressings. An Arbalist needs room to raise the stock, bend her elbows and sight along the bow. - **Splash settings:** a cracked bell tower with the great bell split above her; a belfry in a storm; the cathedral roof at midnight; a bell foundry pit.
- **Light:** dark bronze gloom, a moonlit window behind the bell, rope shadows.
- **Combat tie-in (Toll):** a bronze bell ripple spreads from her at each toll; a Peal is a bigger ring; Midnight is a huge dark-bronze shockwave with pale Sisters rising.
- **Body hook (Zealot Initiative):** silent: she gave up her voice and speaks only by bell; bell-rope scars ring her throat. Show it at every rank; it grows with rank.
- **Dark vow:** ring the hours until the last one.
- **Battlefield mechanic:** Toll counts every hit toward Midnight, which raises the fallen.
- **Do:** bronze and rope, heavy bells, scalloped curves. **Don't:** shiny gold bells, Christmas imagery, bright brass, silver.
