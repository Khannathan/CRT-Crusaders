You are the art lead: reimagine every design, bible and piece of art freely. No existing art is kept. But the game code depends on the things below, so keep them exactly as they are. The full specs live in art-bible/specs/ (locked; they win over every other file).

1. ASSET SPECS: every asset's file name, folder, native pixel size and background in specs/ASSET_SPECS.md. A replacement keeps the existing file's exact size. Real PNGs under 12 MB; .webp only to replace an existing .webp.
2. BATTLE SPRITES: raw drawing about 1024x1536 on flat #FF00FF, one upright full-body figure facing three-quarters right, on the template's lines (crown row 17, top limit 13, soles row 184, 116x172 safe box, spine on centre), core columns 50-78 hold only her body, weapon in the left zone touching her only at the hand, off-hand items in the right zone, arms and legs apart, no magenta in her, nothing see-through. Menu icons pure white on transparent (the game tints them). Splash: head, face and hands clear of the top and bottom crop. Knockout sheets: white and greys on #FF00FF, frame layouts in ASSET_SPECS section 3.

IDS: Sister slot ids (IV-SSR-CEN, IV-LUR, IV-UR1 ...), Order codes (IV AM VT SW CC TC DL GC RP HB), Order ids used in file names (iron-vigil, ashen-mercy, veiled-tribunal, sepulcher-watch, candle-choir, thorn-covenant, drowned-litany, glass-confession, red-pilgrimage, hollow-bell), class ids and codes, enemy, boss, item, skill and effect ids.
