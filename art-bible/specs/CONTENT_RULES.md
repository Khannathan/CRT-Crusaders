# Content rules (Aaron's standing rules)

Aaron decided these. ChatGPT reimagines everything else about the look. To change one, ask Aaron.

## 1. Always

- **Adults only:** every Sister is a young adult woman aged **21 to 35**, and clearly reads as one. Her exact age is in her card's GAME DATA block.
- **Fanservice stays non-explicit:** gacha appeal is welcome, nudity and explicit content are not.
- **No modern or future technology:** no guns, motors, electronics or sci-fi gear. Medieval craft is fine (forged steel, crossbows, siege engines, bells, censers, stained glass).

## 2. The rank appearance ladder

Rank is rarity, and every step up must be a **big visual leap**. The concept below is fixed. How each tier looks in detail (materials, motifs, wings, halos, per-Order versions) is ChatGPT's to design.

| Rarity | Rank | What she is | The step up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R | Commander | a mortal war leader | battle-scarred gear, a commander's presence; no divine features |
| SR | Legend | a mortal hero whose deeds are sung | legendary named gear, trophies, a faint aura; still no halo |
| SSR | Demigoddess | half-divine | clearly divine for the first time: god-forged gear, first wings and a partial halo |
| UR | Deity | a goddess of her Order | divine in full: impossible materials, full halo or crown, many wings; she floats in her splash |
| Limited UR | Patron Deity | the highest god of her Order | everything a Deity has, bigger, plus a mark no other Sister of her Order has |

Divine features grow with rank (the "Wrathful Host" idea: the closer to her god, the more she looks like a terrible angel), while her face stays human. **Exception:** the Confessors of Glass are faceless at every rank: their heads are glass, stained glass or mirror, and their mortal tiers may show chips, cracks and repairs. Everything else here still applies to them (adult age, rank steps, sprite registration, an opaque sprite silhouette). Full godhood is staged in the splash; on the battle sprite, wings and halos stay folded and small (specs/SPRITE_SPEC.md).

## 3. Where the game is today (context only, not a rule)

So you know where the theme stands before you reimagine it. None of this binds you except what sections 1-2 say.

- **Premise:** a dark-fantasy gacha RPG for portrait phones. Sisters are fanatic zealots sworn to 10 rival Orders, fighting a war without end through crypts of undead clergy, drowned congregations and blasphemous saints. The Orders are not good: each god demands something terrible and its Sisters obey. Orders feud, purge heretics and burn villages "to save them". More Inquisition than Church: victories feel like executions.
- **Tone:** grimdark, gothic, fanatical and doomed, with gacha glamour on top. Soot, wax, bone, blood and firelight; fire is the main light. Gods are terrifying first, beautiful second. Never goofy.
- **Game text voice** (Claude keeps it; docs/VOICE.md): a "zealous sermon": fervent, second person, short oaths and threats. Words like sworn, consecrated, by decree, purge.
- **Current art style:** PC-98 (late-1980s Japanese computer games: crisp pixels, anime dark-fantasy heroines, 16 colours, heavy dither). You may keep it or propose another; final files still have to read at native size (LOCKS.md item 3).
- **Orders:** Iron Vigil (dark crusader-knights), Ashen Mercy (pyres and mercy-killing), Veiled Tribunal (judges), Sepulcher Watch (ossuary guards), Choir of the Pyre (burning hymns), Verdant Martyrdom (briar and the lash), Drowned Litany (the deep), Confessors of Glass (faceless glass-and-mirror constructs), Crimson Inquisition (inquisitors and brands), Hallowed Belfry (bells).
- **Classes:** Tank: Hierophant, Sentinel, Martyr. Healer: Confessor, Hospitaller, Abbess. Buffer: Oracle, Cantor, Adjudicator. Melee: War-Sister, Executioner, Veilblade. Ranged: Censer, Arbalist, Exorcist.
