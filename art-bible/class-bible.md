# CRT Crusaders: Role and Class Theme Bible

Use this file with the roster Sister cards, order-bible.md and ASSETS_GUIDE.md. It tells you how each of the 15 classes stands, fights, moves and shows its effects, and how each Order flavours the classes it has.

## Who decides what

| Owner | Decides | File |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sister | face, hair, skin, marks and her weapon | her roster card (wins on anything personal) |
| Class | silhouette, weapon family, armour weight, build, still pose, animation beats, effect shapes | this file |
| Order | cloth colours, metal finish, head motif, motif shape, hem, effect colours, splash place | order-bible.md |
| Rarity | trim, halo, effect count (16 colours at every rank, PC-98 style) | order-bible.md rank table |

So: the **class** says *what shape* a weapon, shield or effect is; the **Order** says *what it is made of and what colour it glows*. If her card names a specific weapon, use it, held the way her class holds weapons.

## Rules for every class

- **Battle sprite:** one raw painting about 1024 x 1536 on flat #FF00FF, standing upright at full height, slight three-quarter turn to the viewer's right, feet shoulder-width or wider near the bottom, belt about halfway down, weapon in her right hand (viewer's left) pointing down and out, nothing across her torso or thighs, both hands and feet fully drawn. Clear shapes, no fine noise. Each card's SPRITE PROMPT gives her rig-safe stance, and on the sprite it wins over the Still pose below (for example the Adjudicator's ledger hangs from her hand, the Arbalist's crossbow hangs down and out, the Executioner's free hand stays off the belt). Full rules and zones: sprite-plan.md. The game shrinks it to 128 x 192 and cuts it to 16 colours itself.
- **The 64 px silhouette test:** fill her black and shrink to 64 px tall. Her class must be nameable from the outline alone. Each class owns one shape word below.
- **Animation:** the game's pixel rig animates idle, run, strike, cast, Litany and hurt from the one battle sprite. No pose sheets or animation sheets are drawn. Each class's Animation beats below tell the rig how she moves.
- **Effects:** always hard pixel clusters in 2-3 values, touching their source. Class = effect shape, Order = effect colour.

## Standing art notes (Aaron, 2026-11-23)

These apply to every Sister and win over anything softer elsewhere in this file.

1. **URs are gods of their Order.** The Limited UR is her Order's Patron Deity, its highest being; the two URs are Deities. SSRs are Demigoddesses. Both must read as clearly divine and far beyond any R or SR (see the rank ladder below).
2. **Full hair, always (Aaron, 2026-11-24).** No Sister has a shaved, undercut, buzzed or tonsured hairstyle: use braids, long or tied-back hair, short crops or hair under veils and helms instead.
2b. **Fanservice, always.** Every Sister is video-game hot and shows cleavage or underboob, built into her Order's habit and her class armour (a plunging sternum cut on a breastplate, a short plate that stops above the ribs, a laced V neckline, a keyhole or underboob cut-out, a corset). Her card names which one. Keep it non-explicit gacha fanservice: no nudity, and every Sister stays a young adult of 21 to 29.
3. **Dark, gritty, occult zeal.** Lean hard into inquisition, crusade, heresy and pyres: scripture tattoos, wax purity seals, heretic chains, brands, blood-scrawled verses, soot and smoke. Every card names one zeal mark; every splash has candle or fire light against black, with blood, wax, chains or smoke in the scene.

4. **Rank ladder: Commander (R) to Legend (SR) to Demigoddess (SSR) to Deity (UR).** Each step is a big visual leap. R and SR are mortal heroes, already formidable; SSR and UR are clearly divine. Her class still sets her armour weight; her rank sets its grandeur. Fanservice and zeal marks scale with rank.

6. **Zealot Initiative (Aaron, 2026-11-24).** Fanservice is turned up to match the boldest gachas: two showpieces per Sister plus a figure-flaunting pose, named on her card (still non-explicit, adults 21-29). The vibe is grimdark fanatic zealots with no modern or future technology (medieval craft only): prayer-etched gothic plate, wax purity seals, consecrated fire from censers, cathedral-sized relics. Confessors of Glass Sisters are faceless glass and mirror constructs (order-bible.md). Every card ends with her KIT: draw her class's weapon and poses so they match her skills and Relic.

5. **Names grow with rank.** Every Sister is her Roman name plus a rank epithet from her card: a war title for a Commander ("Captain of the Lantern Wall"), a famous deed for a Legend ("Who Held the Black Gate"), divine parentage for a Demigoddess ("Daughter of the Unsleeping Flame"), a god-name and domain for a Deity ("the Amber Watcher, Goddess of Walls"). Let the epithet steer the drawing: a Captain of the Lantern Wall carries the wall's lantern; a Daughter of the Unsleeping Flame burns.

7. **The Wrathful Host (Aaron, 2026-11-24).** The Sisters are the Church's living weapons, and the closer to God, the more they look like biblical angels made dark: wheels of eyes, many wings, burning crowns, scripture fire. It grows with rank. **R:** angel iconography only (wing-shaped pauldrons, an eye-in-a-wheel painted on her shield, seraph heraldry). **SR:** first touches (iron-feather pauldrons, a glowing ring-of-eyes sigil, a feather-edged cape, a faint aura). **SSR:** half-transfigured: two wings of her Order's feathers with eyes opening along them, a partial wheel-halo of eyes, scripture fire on her skin. **UR:** seraph-tier: six wings (two framing her head, two spread, two folded low), burning wheels of eyes turning behind her, a crown of flame, tears of gold wax. Her face always stays human and beautiful (Confessors of Glass stays faceless): extra eyes go on wings, halos and armour, never on her face. On the sprite, wings stay folded tight and wheels small behind her head; the splash shows them full.

10. **PC-98 style (Aaron, 2026-11-24): the look that wins over everything.** All game art should look like it came from a late-1980s or early-1990s Japanese PC-98 computer game, never painterly, HD, 3D or modern cel-shaded: crisp hand-placed pixels, anime-styled dark-fantasy heroines with sharp expressive eyes and clean dark linework, and heavy patterned dithering for shading, skin, skies and glows. Every Sister uses exactly 16 colours at every rank, picked from the 4,096-colour PC-98 palette around her Order colours and skin; rank shows through ornament, dithered glows, wings and scale, not extra colours. No scanlines or CRT effects in the art: the game adds them.

11. **Fanatic zealots of rival Orders (Aaron, 2026-11-24).** The Orders are not good. Each Order's god demands something terrible (see "What her god demands" under each Order), and her Sisters obey without question. Orders feud with each other, purge heretics and burn villages "to save them"; loyalty to the Order comes before mercy, kin and even the faith itself. In the art this means less radiant, more burning: wild-eyed devotion, self-inflicted vow scars, ash smeared on face and skin (soot on the glass for Confessors of Glass), fire as the main light source, and smoke, pyres and burned figures behind her in a splash. Deities are terrifying first and beautiful second, though her face stays human and beautiful. Prefer sworn, consecrated, by decree, purge and absolve by fire to holy, righteous and blessed.

# Tank

## Hierophant ("Tower")

**Fantasy:** Bears the reliquary as a shield; Shields and Wards the whole party.

- **Silhouette:** a tall rectangle: she stands beside a shield taller than her torso, topped like a small chapel; tall hood or mitre-like rise to the head.
- **Weapon family:** reliquary shield (a miniature chapel with a tiny shrine or figure inside) + short mace or flanged club.
- **Armour:** heavy plate, layered vestment over it. **Default build:** broad.
- **Still pose:** shield resting upright at her left side, its base near her boot; mace head-down in her right hand; weight even, chin level.
- **Effects:** wards as stepped translucent-looking pillars or panes drawn in 2 solid values; shield shrine light.
- **Vary each Sister by:** shield shrine contents (bone figure, candle, relic) differ per Sister; censer chain or bells hung from the shield; the shrine's carvings.
- **Do: let the shield define her outline. Don't: hold the shield across her chest, make it round, or hide her face behind it.**

**Animation beats**

| Rig move | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| idle | the shrine in her shield flickers (2-3 px light change); she breathes |
| run | heavy march, shield held at her side, no bounce |
| attack | short overhead mace blow, shield stays planted |
| skill | raises the shield one step; a ward shape forms over it |
| litany | plants the shield; a wall of light pillars rises behind the party |
| hit | braces behind the shield, slides back 1-2 px |
| death | kneels against the shield, which stays standing |
| victory | lifts the shield and mace, the shrine shining |

## Sentinel ("Wedge")

**Fantasy:** Plants herself before the tomb; draws every blow and strikes back.

- **Silhouette:** a wedge: widest stance in the cast, a tall shield planted on the floor making a straight edge on one side.
- **Weapon family:** tall coffin or kite shield + longsword, warhammer or oath-bound sword.
- **Armour:** full plate to the feet. **Default build:** broad.
- **Still pose:** shield planted on the floor at her left, rim at shoulder height; weapon point-down in her right hand beside her boot; feet wider than shoulders.
- **Effects:** taunt rings and ground cracks as hard pixel lines.
- **Vary each Sister by:** shield face painted with her Order emblem; vow ribbons or chains on the shield; moss, scratches and dents on the plate.
- **Do: wide, planted, immovable. Don't: lunge, crouch, or carry a small shield.**

**Animation beats**

| run | shield carried at her side, long heavy strides |
| attack | a single short chop or thrust past the shield edge |
| skill | bangs the shield, a taunt ring spreads |
| litany | slams the shield down; the ground cracks into a defensive line |
| hit | leans into the shield, no step back |
| death | falls to one knee behind the planted shield, head bowed |
| victory | raises the weapon high, shield still planted |
