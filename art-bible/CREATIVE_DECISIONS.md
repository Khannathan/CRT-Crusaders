# CRT Crusaders creative decisions

This is Aaron’s record of decisions for the ground-up redesign. Update it when Aaron locks a choice or revises an earlier one. A suggested name, illustration, or detailed pitch stays open until he selects it.

## Order roster

**All seven Order concepts are locked.** The Sovereign Breach and The Dreadful Salutation both join the roster. Rites, lore, and visual details can develop during the art phase.

| Slot | Order | Scope of approval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The Scarlet Succession | Aaron selected the Order built around sacred duels for the right to rule. The victor inherits the office and must answer its next challenger. |
| 2 | The Vesperglass Testament | Aaron selected the Order. All its Sisters are [Vitrali](Races/vitrali/), exclusive to this Order. The Testament guards the barriers and keeps the peace between planes from its mirror travel hub. Aaron approved the rename from The Confessors of Glass. |
| 3 | The See of the Black Noon | An eclipse Order whose Sisters receive divine judgment during totality. Its rites and visual execution will be refined at the art stage. |
| 4 | The Sovereign Breach (rename pending) | Crusader warlords conquer cities by siege and rule the lands they take in their patron’s name. |
| 5 | The Wardens of the Inward Gate (rename pending) | Elite custodians build and garrison monumental medieval fortresses around gates to Oblivion. The inner defenses contain its Heralds and forces. The outer defenses repel cultists and would-be collaborators. Heavy arms and armor define the appearance of every Sister across roles and carry no inherent stat effect. |
| 6 | The Red Office | Demon-hunting templars in tabards and open-faced great helms consecrate their crusades with fire and blood. They give demons no quarter and refuse to hear them speak. Rework the knight-to-saint-to-deity ascent. Divinity should be exceptionally difficult to attain. The means remain open. |
| 7 | The Dreadful Salutation | Terrifying angelic visitations embodied as adult female Sisters. Drop Cherubs from the concept. Develop more original feminine angel forms during the art phase. |

## Creative direction

| Subject | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Premise | Seven rival Orders fight for control of the realm. A greater threat eventually forces them to set aside their feuds. Its identity belongs to the worldbuilding phase. |
| Faith | Build each Order around a patron who demands something terrible. Give heresy, purges, zeal, and blind faith a central place in how the Sisters act. Even the Orders people depend on are morally compromised. |
| Crusade and obedience | Carry holy wars, witch hunts, religious purges, iconoclastic campaigns, forced conversion, and interdictions into upcoming pitches and later redesigns of the selected Orders. Show the darker, grittier consequences of blind faith. Sisters will kill or lay waste on their Order’s command. |
| Tone | Grimdark medieval crusade fantasy in a world already in despair. Draw on *Demon’s Souls*, *Dark Souls*, *Bloodborne*, and *Elden Ring* for atmosphere. Give the Orders enough thematic breadth to sustain seven distinct identities. An Order can pursue its doctrine without a redeeming purpose. Let its rites, material traces, and acts reveal what its Sisters believe. |
| Crusade influences | Draw on the religious fanaticism of *Warhammer 40,000*. Create original names, factions, and iconography. Keep the world medieval and exclude modern and futuristic technology. |
| Roster | Seven Orders. The revised roster size and per-Order counts are open. Every Sister is an adult woman aged 21–35, taller than 6′0″ and at most 7′2″. Establish her own proportions, face shape, personal markings or inlays, and hairstyle before armor. |
| Character appeal | Reserve fanservice for Sisters. Aim high while keeping it non-explicit. The Order’s faith and identity take visual priority. |
| Art direction | Midnight Vigil is the selected Sister illustration style within Retro Gacha, EGA Dungeon Crawler. It pairs expressive adult anime faces and bold pixel shapes with gothic medieval settings, deep shadows, and light motivated by the scene. Use the revised *Moonlit Duel, Firelit Reliquary* diptych as the visual reference. The duelist pinches the seam of her glove. The templar supports the bronze reliquary from beneath. Carry that attention to gesture, weight, and light into each Order’s own designs. |
| Peoples | Humans, Elves, Tieflings, and Constructs form the starting list. The [race register](Races/) records the Vitrali as an approved Construct people. Explore three to five additional peoples during the race phase, with both gacha appeal and a place in the grimdark crusade. |

## Order territory references

Each Order holds its own territory and may extend its operations into another Order’s lands. Establish each homeland’s identity through its architecture, landscape, rites, and everyday objects. Keep territory studies entirely unpopulated, including distant streets, reflections and the far sides of crossings. The approved territory reference will guide later Sister designs.

Review one Order at a time with Aaron. Both the image and the Order lore can change during review. Record approved studies in each Order’s reference collection. [The Undertide Stair](Orders/vesperglass-testament/reference-images/) is the approved homeland reference for The Vesperglass Testament. [The Country Under the Shadow](Orders/see-of-the-black-noon/reference-images/) is the approved homeland reference for The See of the Black Noon. [The Procession at Rest](Orders/sovereign-breach/reference-images/) is the approved homeland reference for The Sovereign Breach (working name). [The Drowned Crown](Orders/wardens-of-the-inward-gate/reference-images/) is the approved Warden territory study. It depicts one gate prison. Warden-only garrisons hold gates in megastructures of varied design, some far larger than the Crown.

## Roles and classes in every Order

Each of the seven Orders fields all five roles and all fifteen classes. Build each Order around a faith, institution, or ambition broad enough to belong to every class. A shared visual language can give a healer and a ranged attacker the same allegiance while preserving their individual weapons, silhouettes, and game functions.

The columns follow Aaron’s order within each role. **Steady** means the class performs the role’s job directly and reliably, with no setup or risk.

| Role | Steady | Greedy | Utility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tank | **Hierophant** shields and Wards the whole party. | **Sentinel** draws every blow and strikes back at each one. | **Martyr** heals by the wounds she deals and rises once from death. |
| Healer | **Confessor** delivers big heals and cures, and rots the foe. | **Hospitaller** heals the whole line at once and revives the fallen. | **Abbess** heals her Sisters with every wound she gives, and dresses them in thorns. |
| Buffer | **Oracle** hastes the party and fills its Zeal. | **Cantor** grants more attack and more crits to every Sister. | **Adjudicator** weakens enemies and drains boss Wrath. |
| Melee DPS | **War-Sister** hits harder the nearer she is to death. | **Executioner** marks a heretic, then finishes accused and wounded foes. | **Veilblade** slips to the back ranks for few but huge crits. |
| Ranged DPS | **Censer** uses smoke that chokes, burns, and weakens every foe. | **Arbalist** fires heavy bolts at one foe at a time, through armor and shields. | **Exorcist** strips the foe’s buffs and scours bosses. |
