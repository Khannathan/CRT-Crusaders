# CRT Crusaders: Game Overview for ChatGPT

Read this to understand the game your art lives in. It explains what the game is and where every image you make appears. For how to draw, follow the project instructions (CHATGPT_PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.txt), ASSETS_GUIDE.md (every asset type's size and delivery), the bibles and the Sister's card.

## 1. What the game is

CRT Crusaders is a dark-fantasy gacha RPG for phones, played in **portrait**. You collect **Sisters**, fanatic zealots sworn to 10 rival Orders, fighting a war without end against heresy. The Orders are not good: each one's god demands something terrible, and her Sisters obey without question. Orders feud with each other, purge heretics and burn villages "to save them". Loyalty to the Order comes before mercy, kin and even the faith itself. You send them into crypts full of undead clergy, drowned congregations and blasphemous saints.

- **Tone:** grimdark, gothic, fanatical and doomed, with gacha glamour on top. More Inquisition than Church: victories feel like executions. The Sisters are gorgeous, wild-eyed and terrifying, with self-inflicted vow scars and ash on their skin. The world around them is soot, wax, bone, blood and firelight; fire is the main light source, and the burned lie behind them. Their gods and Deities are terrifying first and beautiful second. Nothing is goofy or modern.
- **Voice:** every line of game text is a "Zealous sermon": fervent, second person, short, with oaths and threats rather than prayers. Words like sworn, consecrated, by decree, purge and absolve by fire. For example: "The Order wills it. The world can burn."
- **Look:** PC-98: every image should look like it came from a late-1980s or early-1990s Japanese PC-98 computer game. Crisp hand-placed pixels, anime-styled dark-fantasy heroines with sharp expressive eyes and clean dark linework, 16 colours per image picked from a 4,096-colour palette, heavy patterned dithering, dark backgrounds. Never painterly, HD, 3D or modern cel-shaded. An optional CRT-screen filter can be added on top by the game, so never paint scanlines or screen effects into the art.

## 2. The Sisters

- **500 Sisters** in **10 Orders**: Iron Vigil, Ashen Mercy, Veiled Tribunal, Sepulcher Watch, Choir of the Pyre, Verdant Martyrdom, Drowned Litany, Confessors of Glass, Crimson Inquisition and Hallowed Belfry. Each Order has its own colours, headwear, materials, wing material and creed (see order-bible.md).
- **15 classes in 5 roles:**
  - **Tank:** Hierophant, Sentinel, Martyr
  - **Healer:** Confessor, Hospitaller, Abbess
  - **Buffer:** Oracle, Cantor, Adjudicator
  - **Melee:** War-Sister, Executioner, Veilblade
  - **Ranged:** Censer, Arbalist, Exorcist

  The class decides her silhouette, weapon and how she moves (see class-bible.md).
- **Ranks**, which also mark rarity, from common to rare:
  - **R Commander** and **SR Legend** are mortal heroes.
  - **SSR Demigoddess** is half-divine.
  - **UR Deity** is a goddess of her Order.
  - Each Order has one **Limited UR**, its Patron Deity, who appears only on a time-limited banner, plus two **Limited SSRs** tied to her.

  Each rank must look like a big step up from the one below.
- **Names:** a Sister gives up her birth name when she swears to her Order. Each Order names its Sisters from its own culture plus a harsh Latin byname for what she does or what was done to her, such as "Fredegund Turrita, Captain of the Lantern Wall". The closer to her god, the stranger the name: SSRs take a vow-word as a first name ("Excubia Catenata"), and URs go by one god-name ("Insomnia"). Every name ends with a rank epithet. Her name, epithet and named weapon are part of her identity.
- **Kits:** every Sister has her own skills, a passive, a Relic (a consecrated item in her kit; it is not drawn on her, except an Oracle's floating omen relic) and a Litany, her ultimate. Her card's KIT tells you how her skills look, so her art should promise those powers.

## 3. How you play

The game has five tabs along the bottom of the screen.

- **Sanctum (home):** a full-screen lobby. The current leader Sister's splash art fills the background. A strip shows the **Vigil**, a small live auto-battle where her party fights on its own, even while you're away.
- **Sisters:** the roster grid, each Sister's page (her figure, splash, kit, gear and bond), the armoury, war hounds and collections. Bonds unlock short confessions in her own voice.
- **Crusade (centre):** the main adventure. The Road is a chain of stages. The Crypts are branching dungeon maps you climb one room at a time. Daily Labors, the Contest (leagues) and the Abyss (big bosses) are other modes.
- **Altar:** the gacha. You pull Sisters from banners. A UR is guaranteed by pull 80, and a featured Sister stands on the banner as her sprite, with her splash behind her.
- **Market:** the shop.

**Combat** is real time with free movement, viewed side-on. Up to 4 Sisters walk into a battlefield and fight crypt horrors. They move, strike and cast on their own. The player taps to fire each Sister's **Litany** when her Zeal is full. Enemy bosses unleash **Blasphemies**. Fights last about 30 to 45 seconds.

## 4b. Other game art

Besides Sisters, the game uses enemy portraits and battle figures, boss Blasphemy poses, each Order's crucifix icon, insignia and banner, backgrounds, event cards, item, relic, skill, keyword and map icons, menu icons, effect sprites and UI skins. ASSETS_GUIDE.md lists each one's file name, size and background.

## 5. Gacha presentation

A Sister is first seen on the **pull reveal**. The screen flashes her rank colour, then shows her splash, or her sprite at 2× if there's no splash. URs and Patron Deities get the biggest reveal, so their splash must feel like a god appearing. Commanders and Legends should still look like heroes worth owning. Players see each Sister many times as a small sprite in battle, so her silhouette and colours have to identify her at a glance among 499 others.

## 6. Don'ts

- No game interface, frames, text, logos, rarity stars or name plates in the art. The game draws all of these itself.
- No other franchise's characters, symbols or designs.
- No modern or future technology: no guns, motors, electronics or sci-fi gear. Medieval craft is fine (forged steel, crossbows, siege engines, bells, censers, stained glass).
- Don't change a Sister's identity between images.
