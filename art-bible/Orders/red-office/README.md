# The Red Office

## Lore

Templars in tabards and open-faced great helms consecrate their demon purges with fire and blood. They give demons no quarter and refuse to hear them speak.

The greatest crusaders may ascend from knighthood to sainthood and ultimately to divinity. Divinity is exceptionally difficult to attain. The means of ascent remain open for refinement.

## Territory

[The Cathedral of the Lifted Roof](reference-images/) is the approved homeland study. Three claw marks tore through the cathedral lead from underneath. The Red Office stripped the roof from nave and transepts. The surrounding town still has its slate roofs. In the cathedral square, the lead lies rolled on wagons under red wax.

Braziers burn in a line from the west door to the altar. Rain washes the ash wards from the newly exposed paving, and the Sisters lay them again after every shower. Elsewhere in the town, salt lines guard thresholds and a blood palm marks the limewash beside the square.
