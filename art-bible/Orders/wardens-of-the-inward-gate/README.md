# The Wardens of the Inward Gate

## Lore

The Wardens are elite custodians of gates to Oblivion. They build vast medieval fortresses around the gates. Inward-facing defenses contain the Heralds and forces of Oblivion. Outward-facing defenses repel cultists and others who would help them enter the realm.

Every Warden bears heavy arms and armor, including healers and support classes. This visual choice carries no inherent stat effect. The Order’s name awaits revision.
