# The See of the Black Noon

## Lore

The Sisters receive divine judgment during the totality of an eclipse. Their office as inquisitors rests on what is revealed to them when the sun is hidden.

## Approved homeland reference

[The Country Under the Shadow](reference-images/country-under-the-shadow.png) shows the See at totality. The moon’s shadow lies across the salt flats and river channels. A causeway runs through it, marked by watch chapels with iron beacon baskets. The far ridges still catch daylight.
