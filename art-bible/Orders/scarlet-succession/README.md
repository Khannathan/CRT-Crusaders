# The Scarlet Succession

## Lore

A sacred duel can transfer the right to rule. The victor takes the office, inherits its obligations, and must answer the next challenger.

**Territory and court culture.** The Order holds lands befitting a ruling nobility. Rank is worth killing for. Its aristocrats pursue advancement through formal duels, private betrayal, and backstabbing. A challenge makes ambition public. A noble may have already betrayed an ally before either duelist enters the court.

## Naming a second

Every titled noble names a second. If the noble dies before reaching the dueling ground, the second inherits the right to issue that noble’s next challenge. Naming a second is the highest public expression of trust between two houses. It also tells an ambitious friend exactly what the noble’s death would give them.
