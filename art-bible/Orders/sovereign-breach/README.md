# The Marching Throne

## Lore

The Sisters are crusader warlords who lay siege to cities in their patron’s name. After the walls fall they rule the lands they have conquered.

## Approved homeland reference

[The Procession at Rest](reference-images/procession-at-rest.png) shows four siege towers standing where the last uphill march ended. Each serves as a parish chapel. Houses have grown up against the wheels, and a plum tree blossoms through the nearest axle.
