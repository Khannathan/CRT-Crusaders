# The Host of Fearful Grace

## Lore

The Order embodies the terror of an angelic visitation. Even traditionally nonhumanoid angel forms are reimagined as clearly adult female Sisters.

Develop original angel forms for this Order during art review. Cherubs are excluded.

## Territory and rites

Two approved Midnight Vigil studies establish the homeland. The fracture belongs to the golden halo. The Order builds its black spires whole, shaping buttresses, stalls and arches like folded flight feathers.

At [The Close of the Empty Halo](reference-images/#the-close-of-the-empty-halo), the halo rests in a bed of black sand. The processional stair leads through the opening in the gold. At dusk a Sister descends to light one lamp where the angel’s head once rested.

At [The Choir of the Unanswered Note](reference-images/#the-choir-of-the-unanswered-note), four bronze chains hold the fractured halo upright between twin bell towers. At a Sister’s funeral, the western bell tolls for her passing and the eastern bell answers for her homecoming. The hook beneath the halo hangs empty. Its note belongs to the angel.
