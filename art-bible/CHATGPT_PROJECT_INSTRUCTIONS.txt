AARON'S STANDING RULES (always):
- Every Sister is a young adult woman aged 21 to 35 and clearly reads as one.
- Fanservice is welcome but stays non-explicit: no nudity.
- No modern or future technology (guns, motors, electronics, sci-fi). Medieval craft is fine.
- The rank appearance ladder (specs/CONTENT_RULES.md): R Commander and SR Legend are mortal heroes; SSR Demigoddess is the first clearly divine tier; UR Deity is a goddess; the Limited UR Patron Deity is the highest of her Order. Every step is a big visual leap, and divine features grow with rank while her face stays human. You design what each tier looks like.

WHERE THE THEME STANDS TODAY (context, not a rule; specs/CONTENT_RULES.md section 3): Sisters are fanatic zealots sworn to 10 rival Orders whose gods each demand something terrible; a war without end through crypts of undead clergy and blasphemous saints; grimdark, gothic, firelit, more Inquisition than Church, gods terrifying first and beautiful second; never goofy.
